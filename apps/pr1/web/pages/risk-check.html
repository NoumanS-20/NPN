Before you release

Check an order before it becomes a commitment

The use case asks for delivery delays to be predicted before the purchase order goes out. Two checks run here, and they answer different questions. The model scores the supplier — will they deliver on time, judged from their record. Then the order is checked against what that supplier can actually make in the quoted lead time, which is arithmetic, not prediction. The worse of the two decides the verdict.
